Savor of Baltistan — Pure Himalayan Shilajit

Savor of Baltistan was created to share the natural goodness of high-altitude Himalayan Shilajit. Harvested from mountain rocks at high elevations, Shilajit (Salajeet) is a traditional natural substance known for its rich mineral content and fulvic acid.

As the founder, I manage this venture from end to end—connecting with ethical collectors in Baltistan, handling packaging, learning e-commerce workflows, creating educational content, and growing our audience across Facebook and Instagram.
